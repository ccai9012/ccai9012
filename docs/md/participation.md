
# A.G: General Participation

<div style="height:1.5rem"></div>

General participation reflects your **ongoing engagement with the course** through attendance, discussions, activities, and peer review.

<div style="height:1.5rem"></div>

---

<div style="height:1.5rem"></div>

## Attendance

Attendance consists of two parts:

- **Lecture Attendance — 1 point**
    - Attendance will be recorded from **Week 3 onwards**.

- **Tutorial Attendance — 2 points**
    - Students should attend at least **8 of the 10 tutorials**.
    - Each student should take part in at least one **Crit-Style Peer Review**, where you present your project individually to another student in your tutorial session.

For both lectures and tutorials, attendance will normally be taken **during the middle of the class**. We will share a **QR code linking to a class photo, where you can enter your name beside yourself in the picture**.

If you cannot attend, no problem — please send us a short note and we will try to work something out together. Where relevant, attach any supporting material, and please include [mayelhadidi@connect.hku.hk](mailto:mayelhadidi@connect.hku.hk) in the email.

## Engagement

We consider:

- Asking or answering questions
- Contributing to class discussions
- Participating in Mentimeter activities
- Giving and receiving peer feedback

Where participation is recorded, we may ask you to provide your **name**.
