# Part II: Responsibility & AI
## *Make AI responsible to you*
*A Two-Class Module on AI, Society, and Responsible Use*

---

## What This Part Is

Having explored how AI systems work, we now turn to what they mean for us.

AI is not simply a technology that produces outputs. It is increasingly shaping how we work, create, communicate, make decisions, and interact with the world around us.

In this part, we consider the broader implications of AI for individuals and society, including the opportunities it creates, the risks it introduces, and the responsibilities that come with its use.

We look beyond what AI can do, to consider **what it should do, who it serves, and how we can use it responsibly.**
