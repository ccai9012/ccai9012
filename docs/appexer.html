A0: Applied Exercise

Seeing, Questioning & Creating with AI

The Brief

Generative AI can increasingly see, interpret, describe, and transform visual information.

But what does it actually notice?
What does it understand?
What might it miss?

In this Applied Exercise, you will investigate visual artefacts from the built environment, first through your own observation, then through systematic testing of a multimodal AI model, and finally through iterative human–AI transformation.

The exercise moves through:

Human Observation → Machine Interpretation → Human–AI Exploration

Figure 1. Illustrative examples of possible subjects and visual artefacts. Image generated with ChatGPT / OpenAI.

Roadmap

Work through the following pages in order.

1. GATHER

Build a structured visual collection.

Collect approximately 5 collections within your assigned artefact category and document them across:

Context → System → Detail

→ GATHER: Collection & Documentation Instructions

2–3. EXAMINE + IDENTIFY

Look first. Then decide what is worth testing.

Examine your collections yourself, record what you notice and what remains uncertain, and turn those observations into systematic and collection-specific questions.

→ EXAMINE + IDENTIFY: Observation & Question Instructions

4–5. INTERROGATE + EVALUATE

Test what the AI can actually understand.

Use controlled VLM tests, record the first response, interrogate it further, and evaluate its answers against the available evidence.

→ INTERROGATE + EVALUATE: VLM Testing Instructions

6–7. TRANSFORM + SELECT

Move from testing AI to working with AI.

Use selected collections as starting points for iterative transformation, then apply your own criteria and judgement to select the most meaningful outcomes.

→ TRANSFORM + SELECT: Design Exploration Instructions
